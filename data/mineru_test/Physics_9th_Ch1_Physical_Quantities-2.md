## 1.1 Physics and Physical Quantities

Physics is the branch of science that deals with matter, energy and their interactions. Anything that can be measured is called a **physical quantity**. A complete measurement has two parts: a **numerical magnitude** and a **unit**. For example, in the statement “length of the table is 2 m”, 2 is the magnitude and metre (m) is the unit.

Physical quantities are divided into two groups:

• **Base quantities** — quantities that are independent of all other quantities (e.g. length, mass, time).

• **Derived quantities** — quantities obtained by combining base quantities (e.g. area, volume, speed, force).
